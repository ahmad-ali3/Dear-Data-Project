# Dear Data Project

I am a big fan of puzzles and own a small collection of Rubik’s Cubes. However, due to my extracurricular activities and studies, I haven't had much time to get back into speedsolving. I've therefore decided to challenge myself by solving the largest puzzle in my collection, the Megaminx, almost 100 times, with a minimum of 10 solves per day, to see how much I can improve my solving times.
